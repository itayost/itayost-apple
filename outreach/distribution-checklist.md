# Distribution checklist: linkable assets

**Why:** links come to things people want to share. A useful free tool, promoted by hand to the people it helps, is the most reliable way a small site earns them. Apollo Digital's promoted guide got about 12,000 views, 15+ leads and 50+ links. It still sat at position 28 two months later: links take time.

**Asset #1: CRM cost calculator** (status: not built). An interactive Hebrew tool comparing 3-year cost of per-seat SaaS (Monday, HubSpot) with a custom CRM, built on the figures in `content/blog/crm-system-cost-israel-2026.md`. Page: `/tools/crm-cost-calculator` (proposed).

## Before launch

- [ ] Calculator live, mobile-tested, with a short "how the numbers work" section and its sources linked.
- [ ] It links to `/services/crm-systems` and the CRM pillar guide, and they link back to it.
- [ ] One-sentence description for sharing (Hebrew, no hype).
- [ ] Open Graph image that shows the result screen.
