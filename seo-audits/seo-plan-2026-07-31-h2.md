# SEO Plan H2 2026 — itayost.com

**Created:** 2026-07-31, from a grilling session over live GSC, PostHog, and OpenSEO data
**Horizon:** August 2026 - end of January 2027 (6 months)
**Terms used here are defined in `/CONTEXT.md` (Lead, Strong Lead, Organic Lead, SEO Goal).**

## Goal

**15 Organic Leads/month by end of January 2027.** Baseline: ~5/month (PostHog, April-July 2026). Rankings are means, never the goal — the site already holds position 1.7-4 on maintenance-price queries with zero clicks, which proves rank alone pays nothing.

## Strategy decisions (all confirmed 2026-07-31)

| Decision | Choice | Rejected alternatives |
|---|---|---|
| Goal metric | Organic Leads/month | Rank #1 on any keyword |
| Lead definition | Form submit OR WhatsApp click OR phone click, deduped per session | Form-only; manually-qualified conversations |
| Target | 15/month (3x) | 10 (2x), 25+ (5x) |
| Priority cluster | **CRM first**; maintenance + apps get cheap wins only; landing pages abandoned for now | Maintenance-first, apps-first, balanced |
| Itay's capacity | 2-3 hrs/week (review + approve; Claude drafts and builds) | 5-8 hrs; full autopilot |
| Proof | Client case study with real numbers — permission to be obtained | Own-CRM story (fallback); anonymized; none |
| Links | Client-site credits + Israeli directories + 1-2 guest posts/podcasts per quarter | Credits-only; no link building |
| Zero-click defense | Interactive Hebrew calculators + price-in-title snippets | Snippets only; concede price queries |
| Measurement | Weekly GSC/PostHog in the CRO report + monthly OpenSEO deep-dive; pivot checkpoints at months 2 and 4 | Monthly-only, no checkpoints |

## Why CRM-first

- Highest ticket (₪15-50k projects) of any cluster.
- Google already ranks the site 4-10 for בניית מערכת CRM לפי הזמנה variants — demonstrated relevance.
- Competitors (Otherwise, Ryse) are stronger marketers but structurally weak: they implement rented SaaS (Monday/Fireberry per-seat) and cannot sell code ownership. The ownership angle is a durable wedge.
- Head terms currently at 14-28: real headroom, unlike maintenance (already won) or landing pages (60-80, out of reach at domain rank 7).

## Operating loop

- **Weekly (inside the existing CRO report):** Organic Leads by cluster (PostHog: generate_lead + whatsapp_click + phone_click, channel = Organic Search), GSC positions and CTR for the tracked query set.
- **Monthly:** OpenSEO deep-dive — ranked keywords, SERP checks on the CRM head terms, competitor watch (otherwise.co.il, ryse.co.il, idanbenor.co.il).
- **Content guardrails:** Hebrew RTL, first-person singular site voice, no emojis, deslop pass on every draft, Itay reviews everything before publish. Keep CWV green — no regressions for content features.

## Risks

- **AI Overviews expand further** → calculators and case studies are the hedge; commercial לפי הזמנה intent is least exposed.
- **Case-study permission refused** → own-CRM story is pre-approved fallback.
- **Exam season / client crunch** → plan assumes 2-3 hrs/week of Itay's time; everything else is Claude-driven. If a month is lost, push the calendar, don't compress it.
- **Domain authority ceiling** → links workstream is the mitigation; if checkpoint 2 shows authority is the binding constraint, outreach gets the marginal hour.
