# SEO Audit — itayost.com
**Type:** Full site audit, focused on search-intent vs content framing
**Generated:** 2026-07-12
**Data sources:** Google Search Console (7d + WoW), GA4, site source (`src/config/seo.ts`, `src/data/services.ts`)

## Executive Summary

Your instinct is right, and the search data proves it. Across the last week Search Console recorded roughly 1,725 impressions and not a single one of your top queries mentions a technology — no "Next.js", no "React", no "Tailwind", no "React Native". People search for outcomes and prices: *"כמה עולה תחזוקת אתר"*, *"בניית מערכת CRM"*, *"כמה עולה לפתח אפליקציה"*, *"crm vs excel"*. Yet several of your most important page titles and opening lines lead with the stack — the web-development page title is literally *"פיתוח אתרים | Next.js, React, אתרים מהירים ומאובטחים"* and the mobile page leads with *"React Native לiOS ו-Android"*. You are ranking in front of buyers and then greeting them with engineering vocabulary they did not search for and mostly do not care about.

The good news: this is a framing problem, not a foundation problem. Your taglines (the visible H1-level promises) are already outcome-led and strong — *"אתר מהיר שמביא לקוחות, לא סתם כרטיס ביקור דיגיטלי"*, *"חנות אונליין שעובדת בשבילכם 24/7"*. The technical SEO is solid. The gap is in the layer buyers actually see in Google (title + meta description) and in the first 100 words of each service page, where stack names crowd out the outcome and the price.

Top three priorities, in order of impact: (1) rewrite title tags and meta descriptions to lead with outcome and price, moving stack names out of the title entirely; (2) capture the click-through you have already earned — *"כמה עולה תחזוקת אתר"* sits at position 1.6 with zero clicks, a pure title/description problem; (3) close the intent gap by leading page bodies with what the customer gets, and demoting the stack to a "why it's built well" trust section lower down. Overall assessment: **strong foundation, needs a messaging realignment at the SERP and above-the-fold layer.**

The pattern is consistent: the buyer is asking "what will this cost me and what will I get?" and the page answers "here is my framework." Nobody comparing web developers in Israel searches for Next.js. They search for price, speed, "brings customers", and local trust. The stack is a *reason to believe*, not a *reason to click* — so it belongs in the body as proof, never in the title.

Note also an inconsistency in `seo.ts`: the homepage has two competing definitions — an outcome-led one (*"מערכות ניהול, אוטומציות ואתרים שעוזרים לעסקים לעבוד חכם יותר. חוסכים לכם שעות עבודה בשבוע"*) and a stack-led one (*"פיתוח אתרים Next.js, מערכות CRM..."*). The stack-led version is what's rendering. Pick the outcome-led one.

## Content Gap Recommendations

**1. A dedicated "כמה עולה" / pricing hub (High priority, moderate effort).**
Why: price queries dominate your impressions (*כמה עולה תחזוקת אתר*, *כמה עולה לפתח אפליקציה*, *בניית אפליקציה מחיר*) and you rank page 1 for many. A central, well-linked pricing hub with real ranges per service — cross-linked to each service page — consolidates authority and directly answers the dominant intent. Format: pillar page + per-service price sections.

**2. "אחזקת אתר" service/landing page (High priority, quick-to-moderate).**
Why: *אחזקת אתר* (pos 12.4) and *כמה עולה תחזוקת אתר* (pos 1.6) show steady demand, but this is currently answered only by a blog post, not a service you can be hired for. A conversion-oriented maintenance page (monthly plans, what's included, price) turns informational rankings into leads.

**3. Rework the crm-vs-excel comparison for depth (Medium priority, moderate).**
Why: *crm vs excel* pulls 25 impressions but ranks position 89 — the intent is strong, the page is thin/under-optimized. A genuine comparison (when to switch, ROI, migration) targeting the consideration stage can climb and feed the CRM service page.

**4. Outcome-led rewrites of service page intros (High priority, quick).**
Why: not a new page — a reframe. Each service page should open with the customer outcome and a price signal, with the stack demoted to a trust section. This is a content gap in *emphasis*, and it's the direct answer to your question.

**5. Invoice/automation content (Medium priority, moderate).**
Why: *אוטומציה לחשבוניות* and related invoice-automation queries appear repeatedly at low positions — an under-served cluster with clear commercial intent for a small-business audience.
