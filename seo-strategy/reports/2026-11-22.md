Notes for the first eligible week (2026-12-16):
- **Maintenance post (rows 1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 7, 10).** Positions 1.6-4 with 0 clicks across about 400 impressions. Its title already states the price range ("כמה עולה תחזוקת אתר? ₪150-₪2,000 לחודש (2026)"). A searcher may get the full answer from the result itself and never click. Test a title and description that promise what the snippet cannot give: the price by site type, and what each tier includes. The CTR test can only start once the 2026-11-18 edits have 28 days of data.
- **CRM service page (rows 3, 8, 9).** This is the CRM-cluster pick for that week. Row 3 is a ctr-gap at position 1.5 on only 34 impressions, so the "13% usual" estimate is uncertain. Rows 8 and 9 (positions 10-12) are the bigger prize: they are the priority-1 targets in the keyword sheet.
- **Website-price queries** (79 queries; about 1,900 of their impressions land on `/blog/website-pricing-guide-2025`, at positions 45-90). This is the largest impression pool on the site, but it is far from page one. It does not pass the striking-distance rule, so it is not a weekly fix. It is in the keyword sheet as candidates. If it holds for 2 months, the monthly run should look at what the top results there offer that the guide lacks.
- **`/about` decay** is 3 clicks, too small to act on.
