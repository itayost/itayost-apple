# itayost.com Marketing Site

The ItayOst business site: Hebrew-first portfolio and lead engine for custom websites, CRM systems, mobile apps, and automations for Israeli small businesses.

## Language

**Lead**:
A contact-intent action on the site: a form submission (`generate_lead`), a WhatsApp click (`whatsapp_click`), or a phone click (`phone_click`), deduplicated per session.
_Avoid_: Conversion, inquiry, contact

**Strong Lead**:
The subset of Leads that are form submissions reaching the CRM — name and message captured.
_Avoid_: Qualified lead (that term is reserved for a conversation that actually happened)

**Organic Lead**:
A Lead whose session channel type is Organic Search (any search engine, not only Google).
_Avoid_: SEO lead, Google lead

**SEO Goal**:
Grow Organic Leads per month. Rankings are a means; the goal metric is never a position.
_Avoid_: "Be number 1", ranking targets as goals

**Cluster**:
A group of search queries with shared intent served by the same page or page set. The four named clusters are Maintenance (תחזוקת אתר), CRM (מערכת CRM מותאמת), Apps (פיתוח אפליקציה), and Landing Pages (דפי נחיתה).
_Avoid_: Keyword group, topic

**Calculator**:
An interactive Hebrew pricing tool on the site (e.g. מחשבון עלות תחזוקת אתר). Exists to win clicks an AI Overview cannot satisfy and to attract links.
_Avoid_: Widget, tool page
